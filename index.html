How it works

When a match starts, Fog Scout reads the map name at the bottom left of the screen and puts the matching callout map on top of the game. After the round the map goes away again.

Text recognition runs on your PC. Map detection and the overlay open no internet connection.

Not signed: SmartScreen shows "More info", then "Run anyway". Asks for administrator rights once. Set DBD to Windowed Fullscreen.

Everything it does

One program for the whole match

The map is the core. Everything else is optional: switch on what you need, the rest stays off.

01In the match

  • Callout map at the banner. Read from the screen when the match starts, gone again after the round.
  • Floors and variants. Midwich floors, rotated maps and layouts like Coal Tower II.
  • Round timer right below the map, and the last round's time afterwards.
  • Waiting card with search time and lobby state while no map is shown.
  • Lobby found. Sound and notification when the search is over.
  • Bloodpoint cap. Tells you when a round no longer adds bloodpoints.

02Overlay cards

  • Layout editor with presets, snapping and a layout per screen size.
  • System values: CPU and GPU temperature and load, VRAM, RAM, FPS via HWiNFO.
  • Today: rounds and play time of the day, the current streak.
  • Music: what Windows is playing, with cover and Spotify "Up next".
  • Inputs: the keys and gamepad buttons you hold, like on stream.
  • Teammates' loadouts: perks, items and offerings read from the pause menu.

03Statistics

  • Every round with map, length, wait, role, killer and server region.
  • Kills, escapes and bloodpoints from your official match data on stats.deadbydaylight.com.
  • Killers, opponents and builds: who you met and what worked.
  • Records, sessions and form by weekday and time of day.
  • Farming and prestige tracked apart, so they do not skew your numbers.
  • Stays on your PC. Export and backup when you want.

04Recordings and clips

  • OBS records every match, each round listed with its map and role.
  • F8 for an instant clip of the last seconds.
  • Clip editor: cut, slow motion, drawings, censor boxes for names, subtitles.
  • Shorts and TikTok in 9:16, montages from several rounds, your own music.
  • Steam screenshots to crop, draw on and censor. The originals stay untouched.
  • YouTube upload if you want it.

05Server

  • Pick your regions from 15 GameLift regions, with ping for each.
  • Connected to: the region and ping of the game server you are on.
  • Allow all puts the Windows hosts file back the way it was.
  • Regions in the statistics: wait and ping per region.

06Comfort

  • Seven languages, following Windows. Map names are translated too.
  • Hotkeys for map, layout, floor, rotation and clips.
  • Tray icon with a coloured dot for the current state.
  • Discord status with map and role, through your local Discord app.
  • Updates from GitHub, checked against a SHA-256 before installing.
  • Backup and restore of settings and rounds in one ZIP.

What it does

Translates the Dead by Daylight chat while you play. It reads any script, including Cyrillic, Korean, Japanese and Chinese, and shows the translation in a small click-through window on top of the game.

A standalone program. It runs next to Fog Scout or without it.

Not signed: SmartScreen shows "More info", then "Run anyway". No administrator rights needed.

How it works

  1. Mark the chat area once. Drag a box around the chat. The area is stored relative to the game window, so it survives a different window size.
  2. Press Start. The program looks at that area twice a second and only reacts when the text has changed.
  3. Read the translation. Language, name, translation and the original in small print appear in the overlay and fade after 25 seconds.

Fair play

Built to stay out of the game

Both tools were designed around one rule: do not interact with Dead by Daylight. They are still unofficial. Behaviour Interactive has not approved map overlays or region tools, so you use them at your own risk.

  • ReadsScreen pixels, the way OBS or Discord screen sharing does, and window information.
  • NeverKeyboard or mouse input to the game, or any automation of game actions.
  • NeverAccess to the game process: no memory reading, no injection.
  • NeverReading or changing game files.
  • NeverHidden information. The callout map is a static community map, with no generators or positions.
  • ChangesOnly if you use the server region feature: the Windows hosts file, which "Allow all" puts back.
  • OptionalOBS, Discord, Spotify, YouTube and the key display are off until you switch them on.
